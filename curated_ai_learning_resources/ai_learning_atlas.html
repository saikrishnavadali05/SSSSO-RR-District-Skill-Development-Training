💡 The potential “curation layer”

Imagine entering: “I know Python, have 1 hour/day, and want an AI Engineer job in 6 months.” Instead of returning hundreds of links, the platform could assemble a personalized route, explain why each resource was chosen, estimate time, identify prerequisites, recommend projects — and tell the learner what to skip.
